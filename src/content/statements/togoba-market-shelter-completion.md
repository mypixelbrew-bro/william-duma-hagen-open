title: "Statement on the completion of Togoba Market shelter construction"
date: 2026-07-03
dateDisplay: "3 July 2026"
excerpt: "Construction of the 12-stall market shelter at Togoba is on track for completion in August. The project provides covered market space for vendors from Wards 28 and 29."
---
The Member for Hagen Open, Hon. William Duma, today confirmed that construction of the Togoba Market shelter is on track for completion in August 2026.

The 12-stall shelter, funded through the 2025 District Services Improvement Program, will provide covered trading space for vendors from Wards 28 and 29 who currently sell produce in the open with no protection from rain or sun.

"Togoba market serves thousands of people every week, and the vendors who work there deserve a proper place to trade," Mr Duma said. "This shelter has been a long-standing request from the ward councillors, and it is now weeks away from completion."

The shelter is being built by Hagen Building Contractors and includes a concrete slab floor, a steel-frame roof structure, and separate sections for produce and dry-goods vendors.

Mr Duma said the district office would work with ward councillors to manage stall allocation once construction is complete, prioritising vendors currently trading in the open around the market site.
