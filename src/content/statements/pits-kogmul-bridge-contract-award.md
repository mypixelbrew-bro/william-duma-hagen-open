The contract for construction of the Pits–Kogmul road bridge has been awarded to Pacific Bridge Corp following a competitive tender process, the office of the Member for Hagen Open announced today.

The bridge will replace a seasonal ford at the Pits–Kogmul crossing that is impassable during heavy rain, restoring year-round vehicle access between Ward 27 and neighbouring Ward 26.

"This crossing cuts off two wards from the rest of the electorate every time there is heavy rain in the catchment," Hon. William Duma said. "A permanent bridge here has been a priority for several terms, and the funding and the contractor are now both in place."

The K 480,000 project is funded through the Provincial Services Improvement Program and will be built to a specification allowing loaded PMV and light-truck traffic. Construction is scheduled to begin in the second quarter of 2026, with completion targeted for late 2026.

Mr Duma said the district office would provide updates on construction progress through this website and through ward councillors.
