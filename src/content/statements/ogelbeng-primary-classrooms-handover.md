Four new classrooms at Ogelbeng Primary School were officially handed over to the school board today, in a ceremony attended by the district education coordinator, ward councillors from Wards 19, 20 and 21, and parents and students from the school.

The classrooms, funded through the 2025 District Services Improvement Program and built by Kumba Construction Ltd, replace bush-material structures that had been in use at the school since 1998.

"For years, upper-primary students at Ogelbeng were taught in a church building six hundred metres from the school because there was no room for them here," Hon. William Duma said at the handover. "That arrangement ends today. Every class at Ogelbeng is now taught on the one campus, in a proper classroom."

The new wing includes a 5,000-litre rainwater tank and a two-drop pit latrine. The school's board chairman thanked the district office for completing the project on schedule and called on the community to maintain the new facilities.

Mr Duma said the handover was one of six classroom projects completed in the electorate over the past twelve months, with further projects scheduled for completion by the end of the current DSIP cycle.
