The office of the Member for Hagen Open has announced a schedule of ward-level consultations to be held across all 40 wards of the electorate between March and June 2026.

The consultations will inform the priorities for the next parliamentary term, including the allocation of District Services Improvement Program and Provincial Services Improvement Program funding from 2027 onward.

"The current term's record shows what gets delivered when a project is properly identified and costed early," Hon. William Duma said. "I want the next term's priorities to come directly from ward councillors and community leaders, not assumptions made in an office in Mount Hagen."

Each ward consultation will be attended by the ward councillor, representatives of the district administration, and the Member's electorate staff. Community members will be invited to raise unfunded or partially completed projects, and to identify new priorities in education, health, roads, water and market infrastructure.

A schedule of consultation dates by ward will be published at the district office and announced through ward councillors in the coming weeks. Findings from the consultations will feed into the Priorities for 2027–2032 published on this website.
