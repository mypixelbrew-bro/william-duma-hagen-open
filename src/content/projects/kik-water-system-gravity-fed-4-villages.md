A gravity-fed water supply system serving four villages in Ward 6, drawing from a protected mountain source above Kik. The system includes intake works, a transmission pipeline, a reticulated distribution network, and public tap stands at each village.

Approximately 1,100 residents now have access to reliable, gravity-fed water within a short walk of their homes, reducing dependence on creek water during the dry season.
