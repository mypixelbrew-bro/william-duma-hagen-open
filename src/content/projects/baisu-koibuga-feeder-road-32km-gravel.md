Grading and gravelling of the 3.2-kilometre feeder road connecting Baisu to Koibuga, improving dry-season and wet-season access between the two wards. Works include drainage culverts at three creek crossings and spot improvements at the steepest sections.

The road links cash-crop farmers in both wards to the main market at Mount Hagen.
