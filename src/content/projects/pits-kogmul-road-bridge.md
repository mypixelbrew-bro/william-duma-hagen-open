Construction of a permanent single-lane bridge across the Pits–Kogmul crossing, replacing a seasonal ford that is impassable during heavy rain.

The bridge will restore year-round vehicle access between Ward 27 and neighbouring Ward 26, and is being built to a specification that allows loaded PMV and light-truck traffic.
