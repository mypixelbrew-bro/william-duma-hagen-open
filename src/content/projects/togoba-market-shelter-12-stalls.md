Construction of a 12-stall covered market shelter at Togoba, providing weatherproof trading space for vendors from Wards 28 and 29. The shelter replaces informal roadside trading and includes a concrete slab floor, a steel-frame roof structure, and dedicated space for produce and dry-goods vendors.

Construction began in early 2026 and is scheduled for completion in August 2026.
